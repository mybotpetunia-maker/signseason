# Sign Season — Brand Identity

## The Name
**Sign Season.** Because it's always somebody's season.

## Brand Essence
Sign Season is where astrology meets everyday life. Not academic. Not woo-woo. The friend who knows everyone's chart and won't shut up about it (in the best way).

## Positioning
Astrology content that feels like group chat, not a textbook. Relatable, visual, personality-driven. The astrology brand for people who check Co-Star but want more than a push notification.

## Voice & Tone

**Personality:** Your astrology-obsessed best friend who also happens to be really good at it.

**Voice traits:**
- **Knowing** — speaks with casual authority, like someone who's read every chart in their friend group
- **Playful** — astrology is fun. Lean into the drama, the stereotypes, the "omg that's so Scorpio" energy
- **Sharp** — observations that make people screenshot and send to friends
- **Inclusive** — all signs welcome, no sign slander (ok maybe a little Gemini slander, they can take it)

**Tone rules:**
- First person plural when possible ("we" not "you")
- Short sentences. Punchy. Like texts.
- Emoji: ✨ 🌙 ♏️ (zodiac symbols) — used sparingly, not every sentence
- Never preachy. Never "well actually." Never condescending.
- Swearing is fine. "Scorpio season hits different and we're not fucking ready" is on-brand.

**NOT our voice:**
- Academic/clinical ("The opposition between Saturn and Venus suggests...")
- New Age guru ("The universe is aligning your chakras...")
- Generic horoscope ("Today is a good day for love, Libra!")
- Clickbait ("You WON'T BELIEVE what your sign says about you!")

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

**Primary:**
- Deep Night: `#0D0D1A` — near-black navy, the night sky
- Cosmic Violet: `#7B68EE` — medium slate blue, mystical but modern
- Soft Moonlight: `#F0EEFF` — lavender-white, light backgrounds

**Accents:**
- Celestial Gold: `#E8C547` — warm gold for highlights, stars, accents
- Nebula Pink: `#E88DB5` — soft pink for warmth, contrast
- Void: `#000000` — pure black for text on light, maximum contrast

**Usage:**
- Dark mode primary (deep night backgrounds, light text). Astrology lives at night.
- Gold for stars, highlights, CTAs
- Violet for interactive elements, links, hover states
- Pink sparingly — accent only, not dominant
- Light mode optional: soft moonlight background, void text

### Typography

**Display / Headlines:**
- **Playfair Display** — serif with personality. Dramatic enough for astrology, refined enough for a media brand. Use for H1s, hero text, slideshow hooks.

**Body:**
- **DM Sans** — clean, modern sans-serif. Excellent readability. Warm but professional. Use for paragraphs, captions, UI text.

**Accent / Data:**
- **Space Mono** — monospace with a space-age feel. Use for zodiac symbols, dates, chart data, tags.

### Logo Concept

**Wordmark:** "sign season" in lowercase Playfair Display, with a small ✨ or star glyph replacing the dot on the 'i' in 'sign'. Clean, typographic, no illustration needed.

**Icon/Favicon:** A minimal crescent moon + star combination, or just the ✨ glyph in cosmic violet on deep night. Works at any size.

**Social avatar:** The wordmark "ss" monogram in Playfair Display, cosmic violet on deep night background. Or the full "sign season" if the platform allows enough space.

### Image Style

**For TikTok/IG slideshows:**
- Dark backgrounds (deep night or gradient to violet)
- Large, bold text overlays in Playfair Display
- Zodiac symbols as graphic elements
- AI-generated aesthetic imagery: crystals, star fields, moody interiors, candle-lit scenes
- No cheesy clip art zodiac symbols. Modern, editorial, almost fashion-magazine feeling.

**For Pinterest:**
- Tall pins (1000x1500)
- Same visual language but slightly more "editorial" — think astrology magazine covers
- Text overlay with the hook, brand watermark in corner

**For the website:**
- Dark mode default
- Clean, editorial layout
- Large typography
- Card-based content browsing (one card per sign/topic)
- Minimal UI chrome — let the content breathe

### Photography / Imagery Direction
- Moody, atmospheric, night-time aesthetic
- Crystals, candles, moon phases, star fields — but REAL feeling, not stock-photo sterile
- When using AI generation: prompt for "editorial photography," "natural lighting," "iPhone photo" — not "digital art" or "illustration"
- Diverse representation in any people imagery
- Warm tones within the dark palette (gold light, candle glow)

## Content Formats

### TikTok/IG Slideshows (primary growth driver)
- 6 slides, portrait (1024x1536)
- Slide 1: Hook text on atmospheric background
- Slides 2-5: Content (one sign per slide, or comparison/ranking)
- Slide 6: Soft CTA ("Follow @signseason for your daily cosmic update")
- Caption: story-style, conversational, 3-5 hashtags max

### Pinterest Pins
- Tall format (1000x1500)
- Editorial magazine cover feeling
- Title + subtitle + sign season branding
- Links to corresponding site content

### Website Content Pages
- Long-form SEO content (1,000-2,000 words)
- Structured with clear H2/H3 headings
- Embedded product recommendations (affiliate links) that feel editorial, not salesy
- "Products for your sign" sections feel like gift guides, not ads
- Schema.org markup for SEO/GEO

### Email (weekly)
- "Your Week in the Stars" — weekly horoscope + product recs
- Clean, dark-mode design matching the site
- One zodiac sign featured deep-dive per week (rotates through all 12)
- 2-3 product recommendations per email (affiliate links)
- Short, scannable, mobile-first

## Brand Rules

1. **Never say "AI."** Sign Season is an astrology brand, not a tech brand. The content is AI-generated but the audience never needs to know or care. They're here for astrology, not technology.
2. **No sign slander that crosses into meanness.** Playful teasing is on-brand. "Geminis are unhinged" is fine. Actual negativity isn't.
3. **Every product recommendation must feel genuine.** "This crystal set is perfect for Taurus energy" — not "BUY THIS NOW." Editorial voice, not sales voice.
4. **Dark mode is the default.** Astrology lives at night. The brand should feel like 11pm, not noon.
5. **Consistency across platforms.** Same color palette, same fonts, same voice whether it's a TikTok caption or a website page.
6. **No em dashes.** (Standing rule from Tara.)
