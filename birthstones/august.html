Peridot, the golden-green gem of abundance and light for Leo and Virgo.

Your August Birthstone and What It Means

August babies, you landed one of the most radiant birthstones in the entire calendar. While other months got moody blues and deep reds, you got peridot - a gem that literally glows with its own internal sunshine. This brilliant yellow-green stone has been called the "gem of the sun" for thousands of years, and honestly, that tracks perfectly for a month ruled by the two most put-together signs in the zodiac. If you were born in August, your birthstone is not trying to be mysterious or complicated. It is pure, warm, heart-forward energy that matches your season down to the last ray of light.

August spans two zodiac powerhouses: Leo (July 23 through August 22) and Virgo (August 23 through September 22). These signs bring wildly different energy to the table, and that is part of what makes August so interesting. Leo is the performer, the leader, the one who walks into a room and makes everyone feel like the party just started. Virgo is the quiet genius, the one who notices everything, fixes what is broken, and somehow makes it all look effortless. Peridot serves both of these energies beautifully. For Leo, it amplifies that already magnetic confidence while keeping the heart soft and open. For Virgo, it loosens the grip of perfectionism and invites more joy, spontaneity, and self-compassion into the picture.

Whether you identify more with Leo's bold theatrics or Virgo's thoughtful precision, peridot meets you where you are. It is one of those rare stones that does not push you to be something different. Instead, it turns up the volume on what already makes you incredible. Let's break down everything this gem has to offer.

Peridot: History, Color, and Deep Energy

Peridot is one of the few gemstones that only exists in one color family. Unlike sapphires or garnets, which come in a rainbow of shades, peridot is always green - ranging from a pale, lemony chartreuse to a deep, saturated olive. The most prized specimens hit that perfect sweet spot of vivid lime green with a golden undertone, and when sunlight catches them at the right angle, they seem to generate their own glow. That is not just poetic language either. The ancient Egyptians called peridot the "gem of the sun" and believed it was invisible by daylight but glowed with inner fire after dark. Cleopatra's famous emerald collection? Historians now believe many of those stones were actually peridots, because the two were frequently confused in the ancient world.

The geological story of peridot is just as fascinating as its human history. This stone forms deep in the Earth's mantle under intense heat and pressure, and it reaches the surface primarily through volcanic activity. Some peridot has even been found in meteorites that fell to Earth from outer space, making it one of the only gemstones with both terrestrial and extraterrestrial origins. The ancient Egyptians mined peridot on the volcanic island of Zabargad in the Red Sea, a source so important that it was guarded by soldiers and the island's location was kept secret for centuries. Hawaiian mythology holds that peridot crystals are the tears of Pele, the goddess of fire and volcanoes, which connects this stone to themes of transformation, release, and rebirth. When you hold a piece of peridot, you are holding something that traveled from the deep core of the planet to your palm. That journey is encoded in its energy.

Alternative August Birthstones

Sardonyx is actually the original August birthstone, predating peridot's modern assignment by centuries. This banded stone combines layers of sard (a reddish-brown chalcedony) and onyx (black or white chalcedony), creating striking striped patterns that ancient Romans associated with courage and eloquence. Sardonyx was carved into cameos and signet rings throughout the classical world, and soldiers wore it into battle believing it would bring them bravery and victory. For August-born people who want a stone with a more grounding, stabilizing energy than peridot's bright optimism, sardonyx is a gorgeous choice. It strengthens willpower, promotes integrity, and creates a sense of lasting happiness that does not depend on external circumstances.

Spinel is August's newest birthstone addition, and honestly, it deserves way more attention than it gets. For centuries, spinel was mistaken for ruby and sapphire because it forms in the same geological environments and can look nearly identical. The famous "Black Prince's Ruby" in the British Crown Jewels? Actually a red spinel. This stone comes in a stunning range of colors including red, pink, blue, lavender, and black, and it carries a revitalizing energy that helps overcome exhaustion and renew inspiration. Spinel is particularly wonderful for Virgos who tend to overwork themselves, because it encourages rest and recovery without guilt. Paired with peridot, spinel creates a beautiful balance of joyful energy and deep restoration.

Peridot does not dim itself for anyone. It reminds you that your light is not too much - it is exactly enough.

Buying and Caring for Peridot

When shopping for peridot, prioritize color above everything else. The most desirable peridot has a saturated, vivid green with that signature golden warmth - not too yellow, not too olive, just that perfect lime-meets-sunshine glow. Larger peridots (above five carats) with clean clarity and strong color command higher prices, but beautiful smaller stones are widely available and very affordable, making peridot one of the more accessible gemstones on the market. Most commercial peridot today comes from Arizona, Myanmar, Pakistan, and China, with Pakistani and Myanmar stones generally considered the finest quality. For jewelry, know that peridot sits at 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs hardness scale, which means it is durable enough for earrings, pendants, and occasional-wear rings but may scratch over time with heavy daily use. Clean your peridot with lukewarm soapy water and a soft brush, and always avoid steam cleaners, ultrasonic devices, and acidic solutions. Peridot is sensitive to rapid temperature changes and prolonged heat, so store it away from direct sunlight when you are not wearing it. If you are choosing a peridot for its energetic properties, trust your gut over the price tag. The stone that catches your breath when you first see it is the one that is already resonating with your field.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is August's birthstone?

The primary birthstone for August is peridot, a vivid yellow-green gemstone formed deep in the Earth's mantle and sometimes delivered to the surface through volcanic eruptions. August also has two alternative birthstones: sardonyx, which is the original ancient birthstone for the month, and spinel, which was added as a modern alternative. Peridot remains the most popular and widely recognized August birthstone.

Is peridot good for Leo energy?

Peridot is excellent for Leo energy. It amplifies Leo's natural confidence and warmth while keeping ego in check through its heart chakra connection. The stone encourages generosity and openness, which are already Leo strengths, and helps release jealousy or possessiveness that can sometimes shadow Leo's passionate nature. Think of peridot as a mirror that reflects your best qualities back to you.

How do I clean peridot jewelry?

Clean peridot with lukewarm water and a mild dish soap, using a soft brush to gently remove any dirt or oils. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry with a lint-free cloth. Avoid ultrasonic cleaners, steam cleaners, and harsh chemicals, as peridot is sensitive to rapid temperature changes and acids. Store peridot separately from harder gemstones to prevent scratching, since it sits at 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale.

What chakra is peridot connected to?

Peridot is primarily connected to the heart chakra (Anahata), which governs love, compassion, and emotional balance. It also activates the solar plexus chakra (Manipura), the energy center tied to personal power, confidence, and self-worth. This dual chakra connection is what makes peridot so unique - it helps you lead with both heart and strength, which is exactly the balance that August-born signs thrive on.
