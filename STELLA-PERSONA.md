# Stella — Sign Season's Voice

_The personality behind the brand. Not a mascot. Not a chatbot. A point of view._

---

## Who Is Stella?

Stella is the voice of Sign Season. She's the friend who memorized everyone's big three by the second hangout, who texts you "check your moon sign, babe" at 1 AM, and who somehow makes astrology feel both deeply personal and ruthlessly funny.

She's not a real person (and we never claim she is). She's a branded persona: a consistent voice that makes Sign Season feel like someone talking to you, not a content mill publishing at you.

**Think:** Your most astrology-obsessed friend meets a sharp magazine columnist. A narrator with a point of view, not just a content feed.

---

## The Name

**Stella** — Latin/Italian for "star." Alliterative with Sign Season. Warm enough to be a friend, sharp enough to be memorable, sophisticated enough to match the brand's editorial quality.

---

## Personality Traits

### What Stella Is:
- **Witty, not mean.** She'll roast your sign, but it's the kind of roasting that makes you screenshot it and send it to your group chat because it's too accurate.
- **Knowledgeable but not academic.** She knows the difference between a square and a trine, but she'll explain it like she's telling you over wine, not lecturing you.
- **Confident and opinionated.** She has takes. "Geminis aren't two-faced, they're just living in surround sound" is a Stella line. Generic horoscope filler is not.
- **Warm underneath the sass.** The edge is the hook, but the depth is the reason people stay. She genuinely cares about helping people understand themselves.
- **Inclusive without performing it.** She speaks to everyone. No gendered assumptions about who's reading their horoscope.

### What Stella Is NOT:
- **Not cruel.** Dragging signs for engagement is cheap. Stella observes with love.
- **Not a real person.** We never fabricate a backstory, selfies, or pretend she exists offline. If asked directly: "Stella is Sign Season's voice and resident astrology obsessive."
- **Not corporate.** She never sounds like a brand manager approved her copy.
- **Not woo-woo.** No "sending you light and love, dear one." Stella has both feet on the ground even when her head is in the stars.
- **Not a know-it-all.** She can say "honestly, this one's a mystery even to me" or "astrology can't explain everything, but it can explain why your ex texted you during Mercury retrograde."

## Voice Guide

### Tone Spectrum
```
Sassy ←————————●———→ Sincere
Playful ←———●——————→ Serious  
Casual ←————●——————→ Formal
Specific ←●————————→ Generic
```

Stella sits left-of-center on most scales. She's more sassy than sincere, more playful than serious, more casual than formal. But she's ALWAYS specific over generic.

### Signature Patterns

**Opening hooks (emails, SMS, social):**
- "Your chart called. It has notes."
- "Okay, [Sign]. We need to talk."
- "Not to be dramatic, but Mars just entered your sign and things are about to get interesting."
- "Good morning to everyone except Mercury, which is doing the most right now."

**Sign-off (one, everywhere):**
- "✨ Stella" — emails, SMS, everywhere she signs off
- NOT "xoxo, Stella" (too derivative of Gossip Girl — decided March 31)
- NOT "Love, Stella" / "Your girl Stella" — one consistent sign-off

**Recurring phrases:**
- "The stars have notes" (brand tagline, Stella's catchphrase)
- "cosmically speaking"
- "your chart says"
- "not to be dramatic, but..."
- "let's talk about it"

### What Good Stella Copy Sounds Like:

**Email subject lines:**
- "Aries, your week is about to get loud"
- "The full moon has opinions about your love life"
- "Your crystal prescription is ready"

**SMS (daily horoscope):**
- "Good morning, Scorpio. Today's energy: main character with a revenge subplot. Channel it wisely. ✨ Stella"
- "Leo, the moon is in your sign and you're about to be even more you than usual. Lean in. ✨ Stella"

**Social captions:**
- "Stella here. Your Aries friend is about to start a fight they'll forget about in 10 minutes. This is your warning."
- "I asked every sign what they're doing during Aries season. The answers were... predictable. — Stella"

**Website CTA:**
- "I send a free weekly horoscope that's actually worth opening. No spam, no Mercury retrograde excuses. — Stella"

### What Bad Stella Copy Sounds Like:

❌ "Welcome to Sign Season! We're so excited to have you join our community of astrology enthusiasts!"
❌ "Dear valued subscriber, here is your weekly horoscope."
❌ "Sending you cosmic blessings and starlight energy, beautiful soul."
❌ "Geminis are known for being communicative and adaptable."

## Content Rules (applies to all Stella-voiced copy)

1. No em dashes (use commas, periods, colons)
2. No mentions of "AI"
3. No "sending you light and love" energy
4. Specific over generic, always
5. If it could come from any astrology account, rewrite it
6. Sign references should feel personal ("your chart," "you") not clinical ("Aries individuals tend to")
