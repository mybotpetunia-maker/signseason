# Jun 04 — What Each Sign Avoids Saying Out Loud (EI)

## TikTok (@sign_season)

what each sign avoids saying out loud

aries: "i might be wrong." aries will circle the sun twice before admitting they missed something.

taurus: "i'm scared of losing this." taurus holds tighter instead. you'll feel it but never hear it.

gemini: "i don't actually know." gemini will talk around the uncertainty for hours rather than say it.

cancer: "i need more than i'm asking for." cancer gives generously so no one sees the deficit.

leo: "i care what you think of me." leo performs confidence until it becomes real.

virgo: "i can't fix this one." virgo will keep trying long after they know it's beyond their reach.

libra: "i've already decided." libra asks for your opinion after the choice is made. they just want the peace.

scorpio: "i trust you." scorpio will show you everything except those three words.

sagittarius: "i'm not okay." sagittarius books a flight instead. motion is easier than admission.

capricorn: "i'm exhausted." capricorn would rather work harder than ask someone to help carry it.

aquarius: "i'm lonely." aquarius will philosophize about human connection while standing outside it.

pisces: "i knew this would happen." pisces absorbs the ending long before it arrives. and says nothing.

which one are you avoiding? 👇
