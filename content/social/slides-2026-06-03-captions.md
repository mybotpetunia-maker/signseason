what each sign looks like when they're done with you

aries stops arguing. the silence isn't peace. it's the exit.

taurus was patient for a long time. then the warmth just turns off. completely.

gemini already has a new best friend. you'll find out through someone else.

cancer stops checking in. no more "did you eat?" no more "you ok?" you'll notice when it's gone.

leo stops performing for you. the spotlight moves. you're no longer the audience.

virgo stops trying to fix things. they don't criticize. they just stop caring enough to.

libra becomes very, very polite. cold-polite. the kind that means nothing.

scorpio deletes you. not just the number. the memories. the version of themselves that knew you.

sagittarius books a trip. tells you about it as if you're not the reason they're leaving.

capricorn removes you from the long-term plan. they don't grieve. they reorganize.

aquarius starts treating you like an interesting case study. observational. detached. clinical.

pisces disappears slowly. first the depth goes. then the replies. then them. entirely.
