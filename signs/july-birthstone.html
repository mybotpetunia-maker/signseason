Why Ruby Belongs to July

July babies, your birthstone is the most valuable colored gemstone in the world. Ruby. The king of gems. A stone so revered that ancient Hindus called it "ratnaraj," literally meaning "king of precious stones," and offered their finest specimens to the god Krishna. In terms of birthstone prestige, you won the lottery.

Ruby is a variety of corundum, the same mineral that gives us sapphire. The only difference is color: when corundum is red, it is ruby. When it is any other color, it is sapphire. That red comes from chromium, the same element responsible for emerald's green, which is one of those cosmic coincidences that feels like the universe is winking at you. At 9 on the Mohs hardness scale, ruby is second only to diamond in durability, which makes it perfect for jewelry you want to wear every single day.

The finest rubies display a color called "pigeon blood," a vivid, saturated red with just a hint of blue that makes the stone seem to glow from within. The best of these come from Myanmar's Mogok Valley, though exceptional stones also come from Mozambique, Madagascar, and Sri Lanka. A top-quality ruby can actually exceed a diamond of the same size in price. In 2015, the "Sunrise Ruby" sold at auction for over $30 million, making it the most expensive colored gemstone ever sold at the time.

Because ruby is so hard and durable, it can handle daily wear without worry, making it an excellent choice for engagement rings or everyday jewelry.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the July birthstone?

The July birthstone is ruby, a red variety of corundum and one of the four precious gemstones. Known as the "king of gems," rubies have been prized for their intense color, hardness, and association with passion, protection, and prosperity.

What zodiac signs are connected to ruby?

Ruby bridges Cancer (Jun 21 - Jul 22) and Leo (Jul 23 - Aug 22). It protects Cancer's sensitive heart while amplifying Leo's natural confidence and leadership energy.

What are ruby's healing properties?

Ruby stimulates the root and heart chakras, boosting vitality, courage, and passionate love. It is known for enhancing motivation, setting protective boundaries, and increasing life force energy.

How can you tell if a ruby is real?

Real rubies display slight color variations and natural inclusions when viewed under magnification. They are extremely hard (9 on Mohs scale) and will scratch glass. A reputable gemological certificate from GIA or similar lab is the most reliable verification.
