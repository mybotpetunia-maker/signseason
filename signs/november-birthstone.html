November Birthstone: Topaz & Citrine

Golden light for the darkening days. Abundance stones for the most transformative month.

✶ ✶ ✶

November's Golden Duo

November babies get two birthstones that look like bottled sunshine, and considering this is the month when daylight starts disappearing in earnest, that feels cosmically intentional. Topaz and citrine share a warm, golden color palette that radiates optimism, abundance, and joy. They are your personal antidote to seasonal darkness, both literal and emotional.

Topaz is the more prestigious of the two, especially in its imperial variety. Imperial topaz displays a rich golden-orange to pinkish-orange color that is genuinely breathtaking. Mined primarily in Brazil's Ouro Preto region, imperial topaz was historically reserved for Russian royalty (hence the name "imperial"), and fine specimens remain among the most valuable colored gemstones. Topaz also comes in blue (the most commercially available variety), pink, and colorless, but the warm golden tones are what make it November's signature stone.

Citrine is a variety of quartz that ranges from pale yellow to deep amber, sometimes with reddish-orange tones called "Madeira citrine" after the wine. Natural citrine is actually quite rare; most commercial citrine is heat-treated amethyst, which turns purple quartz a warm golden color. While crystal purists prefer natural citrine, the heat-treated variety is still considered energetically effective by most practitioners. Citrine earned the nickname "merchant's stone" because shopkeepers would keep it in their cash registers to attract prosperity and protect against theft.

Healing Properties of Topaz and Citrine

Topaz is a stone of intention and manifestation. It helps you clarify what you truly want (not what you think you should want) and then aligns your energy with that goal. Imperial topaz specifically works through the solar plexus and sacral chakras, boosting confidence, creativity, and the personal power needed to turn ideas into reality. It is the stone that says "you deserve this" when your inner critic says otherwise.

Topaz also promotes generosity and abundance in a way that goes beyond material wealth. It encourages joyful giving, warm social connections, and the understanding that prosperity flows most freely when it is shared. Blue topaz, the more widely available variety, activates the throat chakra and supports honest, eloquent communication. Pink topaz connects to the heart and encourages self-love and acceptance.

Citrine is the ultimate positivity stone. It is one of the very few crystals that does not absorb negative energy, which means it never needs cleansing. Instead, it transmutes negativity into positive energy automatically, like a little solar panel for your emotional state. Citrine activates the solar plexus chakra, boosting self-esteem, motivation, and the sunny confidence needed to pursue your goals without apology. It is particularly powerful for entrepreneurs, freelancers, and anyone building something from scratch.

How to Wear and Use November Birthstones

Topaz in gold settings maximizes its warm, solar energy. A topaz pendant over the solar plexus (on a longer chain) directly activates the confidence and manifestation properties. Topaz rings and earrings are also effective, and at hardness 8, topaz handles daily wear well. One care note: topaz has perfect cleavage along one direction, meaning a sharp blow at the right angle can split the stone. Protective settings and reasonable care prevent this from being an issue.

Citrine belongs in your wealth corner (the far left corner from your front door, according to feng shui) to attract financial abundance. In the office, keep a piece on your desk or in your cash drawer. For personal use, wear citrine as a ring or bracelet to maintain that sunny, confident energy throughout the day. Citrine points placed in a grid pattern around your workspace create an energy vortex of productivity and creativity.

Both stones respond well to sunlight for charging, which aligns with their solar nature. A few hours of morning sun revitalizes their energy beautifully. Avoid leaving them in direct sun all day, though, as prolonged UV exposure can fade some topaz varieties. Citrine's self-cleansing nature makes it one of the lowest-maintenance crystals you can own. Place it once and let it work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the November birthstones?

November has two birthstones: topaz and citrine. Topaz comes in many colors with imperial topaz (golden-orange) being the most prized. Citrine is a warm yellow-to-amber variety of quartz known as the "merchant's stone" for its association with prosperity.

What zodiac signs connect to November birthstones?

November's birthstones bridge Scorpio (Oct 23 - Nov 21) and Sagittarius (Nov 22 - Dec 21). Topaz supports Scorpio's transformation with confidence and clarity, while citrine fuels Sagittarius's optimism and adventurous spirit.

What is the difference between topaz and citrine?

Topaz is an aluminum silicate mineral (hardness 8) that comes in many colors. Citrine is a yellow variety of quartz (hardness 7). They are chemically different minerals that share a similar golden color range. Citrine is generally more affordable than imperial topaz.

Does citrine really attract money?

Citrine has been called the "merchant's stone" for centuries, with shopkeepers placing it in cash registers to attract business. While it will not magically deposit money in your account, it promotes the mindset, confidence, and positive energy that support financial success.
