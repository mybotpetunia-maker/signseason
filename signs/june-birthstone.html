June Gets Two Birthstones (Lucky You)

June is one of only three months that gets two official birthstones, and honestly, both of them are extraordinary. Pearl, the classic choice, is the only gemstone created by a living creature. Alexandrite, the modern alternative, is one of the rarest color-changing stones on the planet. If you are a June baby, you get to choose between timeless elegance and mind-bending rarity. Or just claim both. Nobody is going to stop you.

Pearl has been treasured since ancient civilizations first pulled them from the ocean. Unlike every other gemstone that forms in the earth's crust, pearls are born inside oysters and mussels as a response to an irritant. Layer upon layer of nacre builds up over years, eventually creating something luminous and perfect from something uncomfortable. If that is not the most beautiful metaphor for personal growth, nothing is.

Alexandrite is the newer addition to June's lineup, discovered in Russia's Ural Mountains in the 1830s. Named after Tsar Alexander II, this stone shifts dramatically from emerald green in natural light to ruby red under incandescent light. It is sometimes called "emerald by day, ruby by night," and that chameleon quality makes it one of the most fascinating gemstones in existence. Fine alexandrite is rarer than diamond and can cost over $70,000 per carat for the best specimens.

Healing Properties of Pearl and Alexandrite

Pearl is deeply connected to lunar energy and the water element. It nurtures, soothes, and balances emotions the way the moon governs the tides. If you are feeling emotionally turbulent, holding a pearl or wearing one against your skin can bring a sense of calm that feels almost maternal. Pearl is particularly powerful for supporting feminine energy and reproductive health, and it has been used in Ayurvedic medicine for centuries to promote vitality and longevity.

Pearl also promotes sincerity and truth. There is a purity to its energy that does not tolerate pretense. It encourages you to show up as your authentic self, even when vulnerability feels terrifying. In relationships, pearl energy fosters loyalty, trust, and the kind of deep emotional connection that only comes from genuine honesty.

Alexandrite is all about transformation and balance. Its color-changing nature mirrors the human capacity for adaptation, and working with alexandrite supports you during periods of major life change. It strengthens willpower, boosts self-esteem, and helps you find joy even in difficult circumstances. Alexandrite bridges the heart and crown chakras, connecting emotional wisdom with spiritual awareness in a way that promotes integrated, whole-person healing.

How to Wear and Use June Birthstones

Pearls are delicate compared to most gemstones, so they need a little extra care. They are best worn often (your skin's natural oils actually help maintain their luster) but should be the last thing you put on and the first thing you take off. Perfume, hairspray, and chemicals can damage nacre over time. A classic pearl strand is timeless, but single pearl pendants and stud earrings are equally effective for channeling pearl's calming energy.

For energetic work, place a pearl on your third eye during meditation to enhance psychic awareness and dream recall. Sleeping with a pearl under your pillow can promote vivid, meaningful dreams that carry messages from your subconscious. Pearl water (made by placing a pearl near a glass of water overnight) is used in some traditions as a gentle energetic tonic.

Alexandrite, being much harder and more durable, can handle daily wear with ease. Rings, pendants, and earrings all work beautifully. For those who cannot access natural alexandrite (it is genuinely rare and expensive), lab-created alexandrite carries very similar energetic properties at a fraction of the cost. The color-change phenomenon is identical, and many crystal practitioners find no meaningful difference in the stone's vibrational quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the June birthstones?

June has two birthstones: pearl and alexandrite. Pearl is the classic choice, formed inside mollusks, while alexandrite is a rare color-changing chrysoberyl that shifts from green in daylight to red under incandescent light.

What zodiac signs are connected to June birthstones?

June's birthstones bridge Gemini (May 21 - Jun 20) and Cancer (Jun 21 - Jul 22). Pearl especially resonates with Cancer's lunar, nurturing energy, while alexandrite mirrors Gemini's dual nature perfectly.

What are pearl's healing properties?

Pearl promotes emotional balance, nurtures feminine energy, and calms stress and anxiety. It is deeply connected to the moon and water element, supporting intuition and emotional wisdom.

Why is alexandrite so rare and expensive?

Alexandrite requires very specific geological conditions to form, combining chromium with chrysoberyl in a way that creates its famous color-change effect. Fine specimens are rarer than diamonds and can command prices over $70,000 per carat.
